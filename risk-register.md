# Risk Register: Shotistry Platform

| Risk ID | Category | Description | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RSK-001** | AI / Product | Product distortion / hallucinated brand logos during generation | High | Critical | Implement foreground segmentation and mask inpainting (preserving exact customer pixels) rather than raw prompt-only generation. | AI Architect | Mitigated by Design |
| **RSK-002** | Financial | Currency devaluation / inflation volatility in Egypt (EGP) | High | High | Peg EGP plans with periodic indexing against AI API USD costs; set credit limits calibrated to maintain a minimum 70% gross margin. | Business Strategist | Actively Monitored |
| **RSK-003** | Payments | Paymob webhook latency or dropped callbacks leading to missing subscription activations | Medium | High | Implement idempotent webhook processing with database deduplication (`WebhookEvent` entity) and an active fallback polling reconciliation cron. | Payment Engineer | Mitigated by Architecture |
| **RSK-004** | Architecture | Vercel serverless function timeout (15s–60s) during long AI generation tasks | High | High | Asynchronous job processing architecture: client receives immediate `job_id`, generation is processed via background task/queue, client polls or subscribes via SSE. | Solution Architect | Mitigated by Architecture |
| **RSK-005** | Security / Fraud | Concurrent request race conditions draining credits below zero | Medium | High | Atomic database transactions with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` and credit reservation locks prior to AI API invocation. | Backend Architect | Mitigated by Design |
| **RSK-006** | Compliance | KSA PDPL & Egyptian Data Protection Law requirements regarding customer image storage | Medium | Medium | Clear terms of service, strict tenant data isolation, customer data export/deletion workflows, and transparent AI processing disclosures. | Compliance Lead | Documented |
| **RSK-007** | Operational | Paymob regional differences (Egypt Meeza/Wallets vs Saudi Mada/Apple Pay) | Medium | Medium | Unified checkout abstraction layer dynamically presenting available payment channels based on selected merchant market (`EG` vs `SA`). | Payment Engineer | Integrated in Architecture |
| **RSK-008** | AI / Quality | Inadequate user prompt inputs leading to poor aesthetic generation | High | Medium | Curated photoshoot preset catalog and automated prompt synthesizer that transforms basic product tags into high-end commercial photo directives. | Prompt Engineer | Solved by Prompt Engine |
