## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05
### Added
- **Phase 0 & Phase 1 Complete Specifications & Research:**
  - Market research and competitor benchmarking covering Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Pebblely, Photoroom, Flair AI, and Laqta.
  - Business model and financial plan with dual-currency pricing (EGP & SAR) calibrated to a 75%+ gross margin target.
  - Google Gemini & Imagen 3 AI integration architecture (`@google/genai`) with dual-stage masked inpainting for 100% product fidelity.
  - Paymob payment architecture with HMAC-SHA512 webhook security and automated 30-day recurring subscription lifecycle.
  - Relational database schema (Prisma/PostgreSQL) with immutable double-entry credit ledger and atomic reservation locking.
  - Complete REST API specifications and Architecture Decision Records (ADR-001, ADR-002, ADR-003).
  - Superpowers methodology integration in `AGENTS.md`.
  - Comprehensive quality assurance, security, data privacy (KSA PDPL & Egypt Law No. 151), and deployment guides.
