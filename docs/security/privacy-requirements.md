# Data Privacy & Regional Regulatory Compliance (KSA PDPL & Egypt Data Protection)

## 1. Regulatory Context
Shotistry processes commercial and personal data across two jurisdictions:
- **Saudi Arabia:** Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) overseen by the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
- **Egypt:** Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

---

## 2. Customer Asset Ownership & Rights
1. **Merchant Intellectual Property:** The customer retains 100% ownership of all uploaded product assets, logos, brand profiles, and generated commercial images. Shotistry makes no claim of ownership or copyright over merchant creations.
2. **Commercial License:** All images generated through paid plans grant an unrestricted, perpetual, worldwide commercial exploitation license to the merchant.
3. **Data Deletion & Right to be Forgotten:** Merchants can delete products, assets, or close their workspace at any time. S3/R2 storage files and database records are permanently purged within 30 days of account deletion.

---

## 3. Google Gemini API Privacy Terms
Under Google's official paid commercial API terms:
- Data sent to the Gemini API (prompts, uploaded product reference images) via paid developer projects **is NOT used by Google to train or fine-tune models**.
- Customer data is strictly encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- All generated images embed Google's invisible **SynthID digital watermark** for provenance verification.
