# Feature Prioritization & RICE Scoring Framework

We evaluated all potential capabilities using the standard RICE framework:
$$\text{RICE Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$$

- **Reach (R):** Projected monthly active merchants affected (1–10).
- **Impact (I):** Revenue and conversion impact (3 = massive, 2 = high, 1 = medium, 0.5 = low).
- **Confidence (C):** Technical & customer validation certainty (100%, 80%, 50%).
- **Effort (E):** Person-weeks of engineering complexity (1–10).
