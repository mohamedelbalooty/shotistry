# Target Customer Personas

## Persona 1: Tariq Al-Otaibi — The Saudi E-Commerce Merchant (DNVB)

- **Role:** Founder & Operator of an artisanal perfume & oud brand on Salla.
- **Location:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
- **Store Platform:** Salla (سلة), Instagram, Snapchat Ads.
- **Monthly Revenue:** 35,000 – 120,000 SAR.
- **SKU Count:** 18 active perfumes, launches 2–3 new blends each season.
- **Core Challenge:**
  - Launching a new perfume collection requires waiting 3 weeks for an ad agency or professional studio in Riyadh, costing 3,500+ SAR each time.
  - Social ads require fresh creative variations every week to prevent ad fatigue and keep ROAS high.
- **Tech Savviness:** Intermediate. Uses iPhone 15 Pro, manages store via Salla dashboard on iPad and Mac.
- **Preferred Payment:** Apple Pay via Mada card.
- **Key Decision Triggers:**
  - Photorealism of glass bottle reflections and liquid opacity.
  - Regional luxury aesthetic presets (dark stone, gold leaf, desert dunes, marble podiums).
  - Fast turnaround (needs 10 ad creatives within 30 minutes).
- **Willingness to Pay:** 149 – 299 SAR/month without hesitation.

## Persona 2: Nouran El-Shamy — The Egyptian Social Commerce Entrepreneur

- **Role:** Founder & Designer of a handcrafted leather bags & footwear brand.
- **Location:** New Cairo / Maadi, Egypt.
- **Store Platform:** Instagram Shop, TikTok, Facebook Catalog, Shopify.
- **Monthly Revenue:** 80,000 – 250,000 EGP.
- **SKU Count:** 40+ SKUs with frequent seasonal colorway variations.
- **Core Challenge:**
  - Traditional photo sessions in Cairo cost upwards of 8,000 EGP and require hiring models, stylists, and studio time.
  - Foreign card limits prevent her from subscribing to international AI tools billed in USD through Stripe.
- **Tech Savviness:** Moderate. Mobile-first user; creates reels and stories directly on smartphone.
- **Preferred Payment:** Local Egyptian Visa/Mastercard, Meeza, or Vodafone Cash / InstaPay.
- **Key Decision Triggers:**
  - Accurate preservation of leather textures, stitching details, and metal buckles.
  - Affordable EGP pricing with clear return on investment.
  - Intuitive Arabic interface that requires no technical prompting skills.
- **Willingness to Pay:** 399 – 799 EGP/month.

## Persona 3: Karim Hegazy — The Performance Marketing Agency Director

- **Role:** Head of Creative & Media Buying at a performance marketing agency in Cairo & Riyadh.
- **Location:** Cairo (with regional GCC e-commerce clients).
- **Client Base:** Manages 14 e-commerce brands across cosmetics, apparel, and electronics.
- **Monthly Ad Spend Managed:** $60,000+ USD across Meta and TikTok.
- **Core Challenge:**
  - Clients provide poor, flat supplier product photos and expect high-converting ad creatives with tight deadlines.
  - Graphic design team is overwhelmed doing repetitive Photoshop background cutouts and composite retouching.
- **Tech Savviness:** High. Expert in Figma, Photoshop, Ad Manager, and AI generation tools.
- **Preferred Payment:** Corporate debit/credit card with consolidated invoicing.
- **Key Decision Triggers:**
  - Multi-brand workspaces with saved brand assets (logos, color palettes, preferred aspect ratios).
  - High-resolution export (4K / 300 DPI) suitable for social ad placements and catalog printing.
  - Batch generation and team collaboration seats.
- **Willingness to Pay:** 999 – 2,499 EGP / 399 – 899 SAR per month on agency tier.
