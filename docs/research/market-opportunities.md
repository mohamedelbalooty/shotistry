### A. Frictionless Regional Monies (Paymob Advantage)
- Competitors exclusively bill in USD through Stripe, which creates severe barriers in Egypt due to central bank restrictions on foreign currency card quotas, and causes card decline friction in Saudi Arabia where Mada and Apple Pay rule consumer spending.
- By partnering directly with **Paymob**, Shotistry captures the 60%+ of merchants who cannot or will not pay via international USD gateways.

### B. Curated Regional Photoshoot Styles & Seasonal Campaigns
- E-commerce revenue in the MENA region peaks intensely around specific seasonal periods:
  1. **Ramadan & Eid Seasons:** Responsible for 35–45% of annual consumer spending in retail, fashion, food, and perfumes.
  2. **Saudi National Day & Founding Day:** Explosive promotional periods with distinct green/white, heritage, and national themes.
  3. **White Friday (Black Friday):** High-volume promotional campaigns requiring rapid visual asset generation.
- Shotistry provides out-of-the-box, 1-click photoshoot presets tailored specifically to these commercial moments.

### C. Direct Salla, Zid, & Shopify Compatibility
- Export formats customized for regional store standards:
  - 1:1 Square (1080x1080) for Salla/Zid product cards and Instagram feed.
  - 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920) for Instagram Stories, TikTok Shop, and Snapchat ads.
  - 4:5 Portrait (1080x1350) for Meta feeds.
  - 16:9 Landscape for website hero banners.
