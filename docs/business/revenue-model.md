## 3. Key Revenue Drivers & Expansion Vectors
1. **Recurring MRR (Primary Engine):** 80–85% of total revenue derived from automated Paymob 30-day card debits.
2. **Usage Top-Ups (Burst Volume):** 15–20% of revenue driven by seasonal marketing rushes (Ramadan collections, White Friday catalogs).
3. **Low Churn Vectors:**
   - Saved Brand Profiles (logos, color palettes, custom brand prompts) create high switching costs.
   - History of generated catalog assets stored in user workspace prevents platform abandonment.
