# Commercial Product Photography Quality Evaluation Rubric

## 1. Overview & Objective
This rubric establishes the formal criteria for grading synthetic commercial product photography generated by Shotistry. Every delivered photoshoot deliverable is scored across 8 objective dimensions on a 1–5 Likert scale by two independent human evaluators alongside automated pixel-fidelity metrics.

---

## 2. Scoring Dimensions

### Dimension 1: Product Identity Preservation (Weight: 25%)
- **5 (Flawless):** The physical product is identical to the camera capture in proportions, materials, curvature, seams, and tactile texture.
- **4 (Excellent):** Extremely high identity preservation. Minor imperceptible lighting variance that does not change the perceived product.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Subtle smoothing or slight distortion of non-critical edge boundaries, but unmistakably the exact same product.
- **2 (Poor):** Noticeable geometry deformation, warped cap/lid, or altered material sheen (e.g. glass rendered as matte plastic).
- **1 (Reject):** The AI hallucinated a different product model or distorted the product beyond commercial recognition.

### Dimension 2: Label Text & Typography Accuracy (Weight: 25%)
- **5 (Flawless):** Every letter, font weight, spacing, kerning, Arabic diacritic, and brand tagline is 100% legible, crisp, and unwarped.
- **4 (Excellent):** Text is fully readable and accurate; very minor softening on fine subtext under 6pt.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Main brand name and primary product title are crisp; fine ingredients or small subtext slightly blurred.
- **2 (Poor):** Warped letterforms, misspelled brand name, or pseudotext/hallucinated characters.
- **1 (Reject):** Hallucinated gibberish text, obliterated labels, or replaced brand name.

### Dimension 3: Logo & Brand Symbol Invariance (Weight: Hard Gate)
- **PASS:** Brand logo, icon, or trademark crest is 100% preserved with zero shape alteration, inversion, or hallucination.
- **HARD FAIL:** Any alteration to the brand logo, icon, or insignia immediately disqualifies the photoshoot from commercial delivery.

### Dimension 4: Color Fidelity & Texture Materiality (Weight: 15%)
- **5 (Flawless):** Color matches the original phone capture (ΔE < 2.0 on the product body); metallic, glass, leather, or fabric textures feel tactile and real.
- **4 (Excellent):** Slight tone shift consistent with studio warm/cool key lighting (ΔE < 4.0), materials clearly identifiable.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Minor tint or saturation shift (ΔE < 6.0), materials still acceptable for social commerce.
- **2 (Poor):** Substantial color drift (e.g. crimson perfume appears purple or brown).
- **1 (Reject):** Inverted colors, plastic look on natural materials, or complete loss of material texture.

### Dimension 5: Studio Lighting, Shadows & Contact Occlusion (Weight: 15%)
- **5 (Flawless):** Physically accurate contact shadows, soft directional drop shadows, realistic specular highlights matching scene light sources, zero "floating object" artifact.
- **4 (Excellent):** Strong realistic contact shadows and directional light integration; minor ambient reflection discrepancy.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Noticeable contact shadow, object feels grounded, but lighting direction slightly inconsistent with background elements.
- **2 (Poor):** Weak or missing contact shadow; product appears "pasted on" with inconsistent light direction.
- **1 (Reject):** Product visibly floats in mid-air with zero shadow, or light direction directly contradicts the scene.

### Dimension 6: Background Realism & Preset Authenticity (Weight: 10%)
- **5 (Flawless):** Background is ultra-realistic, photorealistic depth-of-field (bokeh), culturally authentic to regional presets (e.g. authentic Gulf marble, Ramadan lanterns, minimalist studio).
- **4 (Excellent):** High-quality background, convincing textures, clean focal separation.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Good background with minor AI generative artifacts in extreme defocus areas.
- **2 (Poor):** Cluttered, distracting background with warped background props or visible AI brush artifacts.
- **1 (Reject):** Nonsensical, surrealist, or culturally inappropriate background elements.

### Dimension 7: Composition & Commercial E-Commerce Utility (Weight: 10%)
- **5 (Flawless):** Ready for high-converting Instagram/TikTok ads, Amazon/Noon/Salla/Shopify hero banners; perfect breathing room and rule of thirds.
- **4 (Excellent):** Strong commercial presentation suitable for direct e-commerce listing.
- **3 (Acceptable):** Usable with minor manual cropping.
- **2 (Poor):** Awkward product placement, product clipped by frame edge, or awkward perspective.
- **1 (Reject):** Unusable for any commercial purpose.

---

## 3. Benchmark Acceptance Thresholds
- **Tier 1 Launch Criterion:** ≥ 80% of generated benchmark photos across all categories must achieve an aggregate score of ≥ 4.0/5.0.
- **Zero Tolerance Policy:** 0 hard-fail logo alterations or brand name hallucinations in the accepted deliverable set.
- **Lock-Product Invariant:** Categories with complex micro-typography or luxury metallic branding must default to `lockProductForeground: true` to mathematically ensure 100% pixel fidelity.
